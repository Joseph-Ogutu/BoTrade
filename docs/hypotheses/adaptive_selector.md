# Hypothesis: adaptive_selector

Generated by scripts/gen_hypotheses.py from the code. Registered before any real-data run (kill-criterion Amendment 4).

## Claim

Recent hit rate of a strategy predicts its next-trade hit rate.

## Rule

Re-scores the three base strategies on already-settled past decisions and follows the best one if its hit rate is at least 0.55 over at least 8 decisions. Duration: 5 ticks. Contracts: CALL and PUT only; the signal decides the direction.
