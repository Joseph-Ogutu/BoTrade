# Hypothesis: rsi_bollinger

Generated by scripts/gen_hypotheses.py from the code. Registered before any real-data run (kill-criterion Amendment 4).

## Claim

After a move outside the Bollinger band with RSI at an extreme, price reverts within 5 ticks often enough to beat break-even.

## Rule

Bollinger band over bb_period ticks (mean +/- bb_k standard deviations) and a simple-average RSI over rsi_period ticks. CALL if price is below the lower band and RSI < rsi_low; PUT if above the upper band and RSI > rsi_high. Duration: 5 ticks. Contracts: CALL and PUT only; the signal decides the direction.
