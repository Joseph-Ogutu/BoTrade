# Plan: looking for an edge on real markets (draft, 2026-11-10)

Written for the owner. Nothing here is registered or running. It becomes a test only when it is added to docs/kill-criterion.md as a new
append-only amendment BEFORE any data is looked at.

## Why change markets

The synthetic indices (R_100, 1HZ100V) are random by construction. Four of four finished forward tests landed at 49.2 to 49.8 percent wins
against a 52.6 percent break-even. Waiting does not move a strategy's true win rate; it only tightens the measurement. If an edge exists
anywhere on this platform it has to be in something that is not generated by a random number generator.

## Honest odds

Low. Real markets (forex, gold, crypto) are priced by many participants and the short horizons used by fixed-payout contracts are the
hardest place to find anything. The payout is about 90 percent, so a strategy needs to win about 53 to 54 percent after multiple-testing
correction. My estimate that a simple rule clears this is a few percent, not a likelihood. UNVERIFIED: which real-market symbols and
contract types Deriv offers on this account, and their payout ratios. Step 1 measures that.

## Steps (each is a gate; stop if one fails)

1. Measure the cost, trade nothing. Read the available symbols and the quoted payout for 5 to 10 minute contracts on a few liquid markets
   (a major forex pair, gold, one crypto if offered). Compute break-even = 1 / (1 + payout ratio). If the payout is below about 0.85 the
   break-even is above 54 percent and the idea is dead on that market. Output: a table, no strategy involved.
2. Collect forward ticks for the chosen markets with the existing collector. Market-hours gaps (weekends for forex) are logged and never
   bridged. No statistic on the collected data until step 4 is registered.
3. Write hypotheses first, each with a reason it should work and the number that would kill it. Candidates (fewer is better, each one
   raises the bar for all the others):
   - H1 Mean reversion after an unusually large move over the last N ticks, because very short-horizon moves in liquid markets partly
     reverse (bid-ask bounce). Kill if the win rate is not above break-even plus margin.
   - H2 Trend continuation only during the most active session of the market, because direction is more persistent when volume is high.
   - H3 No trade near scheduled news and at the open/close, as a filter on H1 or H2 rather than a strategy of its own.
   Parameters are fixed in the registration; none are tuned on the data.
4. Register in a new amendment (variants, parameters, N per variant from required_n at the registered effect size, the correction for the
   number of variants, a random control per market, the single evaluation rule). Then run the same shadow forward test as Amendment 5.
5. Evaluate once per variant at its N. A pass is written to the ledger by the existing job and approves the strategy for the demo and
   real bots through the same path as today.

## What this does not do

It does not look for patterns in the data already collected (that would only find chance fits), does not touch the holdout, and does not
promise a result. The most likely outcome of steps 1 to 5 is the same as the synthetic result: no edge. That is a legitimate answer and it
costs nothing but time.

## Time

Step 1 is about an hour. Steps 2 to 5 need N completed contracts per variant; at one decision per 5 to 10 minutes per market that is weeks,
not days, and forex stops at weekends.
